# Current map art direction — premium dimensional painted world

User verdict2026-11-04: the cel forest test still does not feel premium.

Target: 日系立體手繪冒險場景. Preserve the original four trainer identities,
age and body proportions, including younger Blaze. Match their rich illustrated
material finish, reflected light and selective highlights. Premium quality comes
from designed space, form, material and coherent light—not texture quantity.

User selection2026-11-04: 「不用了,這個風格都可以」 explicitly selects the
premium-forest-direction-v1 image direction. The optional three-style comparison
is cancelled; no additional style variants are requested. Continue the ONE
modular playable crossing under PREMIUM_CROSSING_CONTRACT.md. This direction
selection is not acceptance of an unbuilt playable map or the complete world.

- Fixed orthographic game camera, roughly50–55degrees above the ground.
  Full-frame continuous world, no horizon/tabletop display base/DOF blur.
- Actual readable landform: raised grass/soil banks, exposed earth/stone,
  shallow stream bed and deeper centre, bridge thickness/beams/footings.
- Material-specific painting: cloth/leather/brass on actors; matte vegetation,
  earthy path, rough timber, damp stone and clear water in the world. Moderate
  form gradients and soft reflected color; selective fine edge accents.
- One upper-left key light, cool sky bounce and attached contact/AO shadows.
  Avoid uniform thick black contours, scalloped cloud crowns, exact flat2–3
  bands, plastic toy/clay surfaces, photoreal grass noise and enlarged paintings.
- Quiet resolved ground, richer medium-form structure at tree groups, banks,
  crossings and landmarks. Ordinary small flora remains small. Root/material
  transitions are drawn as part of the same ground, not opaque colored pads.
- One native game-view proof before any further asset batch or region expansion.
  Built-in environment concept must contain no Pokemon; overlay actual existing
  trainer/Pokemon sprites through the game renderer for the style comparison.
  Preserve original sprite identities rather than beautifying them only inside
  a generated scene. Compare current camera and a uniformly closer camera if
  helpful; changing framing is separate from rewriting character proportions.
- A concept/overlay is not a playable map or approval. Reconstruct modular
  terrain and objects, then test the actual route/depth/collision/joins/mobile.
  Existing58×48projection, gameplay and source-density/DPR cache work remain
  reusable. This direction does not authorize an engine migration.
